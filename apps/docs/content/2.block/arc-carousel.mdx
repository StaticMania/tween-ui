A showcase for a handful of pieces, laid out on a curve. The cards **hang on a
downward arc**, each one further from the centre sitting a little lower and
**turned a little more**, three to a side. **Step** and the whole arc swings
round: every card glides to its neighbour's slot on a slow, weighted curve,
while the one arriving in the middle **eases up in size** and takes a **lime
outline**. Cards fade out past the third slot and wrap round unseen, so the
loop never snaps. **Swipe**, **drag**, use the **arrow keys**, or **click a
side card** to bring it to the centre. GSAP with ScrollTrigger.

## Usage

::tabs
:::tabs-item{label="React" icon="i-lucide-atom"}

```tsx [app/page.tsx]
import ArcCarousel from '@/components/tweenui/arc-carousel';

export default function Page() {
  return <ArcCarousel />;
}
```

:::
::

It ships with eight sample projects. Pass your own as `items`, and put your
own heading above it:

```tsx [app/page.tsx]
import ArcCarousel from '@/components/tweenui/arc-carousel';

export default function Page() {
  return (
    <ArcCarousel
      label="New this season"
      items={[
        {
          title: 'Field Jacket',
          tag: 'Outerwear',
          meta: '$240',
          image: '/shop/jacket.jpg',
          href: '/shop/jacket',
        },
        {
          title: 'Day Pack',
          tag: 'Bags',
          meta: '$120',
          image: '/shop/pack.jpg',
          href: '/shop/pack',
        },
        // …
      ]}
    />
  );
}
```

The colors are the Tween UI palette, written inline as Tailwind classes so
there is nothing to add to your theme: the deep ink stage (`#03110f`), card
surfaces in `#071d1b`, a teal glow (`#045f64`), soft teal text (`#9fd4d6`) and
the lime accent (`#c6f56f`) on the centre card and the controls. Restyle them
in the `className` strings.

### Autoplay

Autoplay is on by default: a new card takes the centre every 5 seconds, and a
pause / play button sits beside the arrows with a lime ring filling up as the
current card's time runs out. Set the pace with `interval`, or turn it off with
`autoplay={false}`, which also drops the button:

```tsx [app/page.tsx]
<ArcCarousel interval={4000} />      // a new card every 4s
<ArcCarousel autoplay={false} />     // steps only when the viewer does
```

## Props

| Prop       | Type                | Default               | Description                             |
| ---------- | ------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| `items`    | `ArcCarouselItem[]` | Eight sample projects | The cards on the arc.                   |
| `label`    | `string`            | `'Featured projects'` | Accessible name for the carousel.       |
| `autoplay` | `boolean`           | `true`                | Step to the next card on its own.       |
| `interval` | `number`            | `5000`                | Time each card holds the centre, in ms. |

Each item is `{ title, image, tag?, meta?, href? }`. The `tag` is the chip on
the top left of the card and `meta` the line under the title. With an `href`
the centre card is a link and shows a round arrow. Extends native `<section>`
attributes (`className`, `style`, …).

## Behavior

- **One card size, one arc.** Every card is 312 × 430px and the slots are
  fixed offsets from the centre, so the curve keeps its shape whatever the
  content. Below 1024px of container width the whole arc scales to 80%, and
  below 768px to 62%, so it fits a phone without relaying out.
- **One number drives the arc.** GSAP tweens the carousel's position, and
  every card is placed from it each frame, sliding smoothly between slots.
  Past the third slot a card fades out, so it crosses from one end of the
  loop to the other while invisible. With eight or more items that crossing
  is never seen.
- **Side cards step, the centre card opens.** A click on a side card moves
  the arc one step toward it instead of following its link, and a swipe or
  drag of more than 50px sideways steps it the other way and swallows the
  click that ends it. Vertical swipes still scroll the page.
- **The centre card steps forward.** Side cards sit under a light shade of
  the stage color; the centre card loses it, grows 5%, takes a lime outline
  and turns its arrow lime, so the eye lands on it first.
- **The edges fade, the arc does not stop.** The soft fades on both sides sit
  above the cards, so the outer ones run out into the background instead of
  being cut off by the edge of the section.
- **Light on the browser.** Cards move on `transform` and `opacity` only,
  with no blur. Photos come at 1x and 2x with fixed dimensions; the five
  nearest the centre load first and the rest lazily.
- **Autoplay waits for you.** It holds while the mouse rests on the cards,
  while the cards have keyboard focus and while the block is off-screen, and
  picks up where it left off. The progress ring is the clock: one GSAP
  tween that pauses and resumes. Stepping by hand gives the new card a full
  interval.
- **It is in place before it runs.** The first layout is written into the
  markup, so the arc renders already curved and nothing flashes into place
  on load.

## Accessibility

::note
The carousel is a focusable group with `aria-roledescription="carousel"`,
named by `label`, and the left and right arrow keys step it while it has
focus. Each card is a slide labelled `n of total: title`; cards off the
centre are hidden from screen readers and out of the tab order, so only the
card you are looking at can be reached. The current card is announced
politely after every step. The arrows are real buttons with labels and every
control has a visible lime focus ring. With autoplay on there is always a
labelled pause button, and the announcement goes quiet while the carousel plays
itself, so a screen reader is not interrupted every few seconds. Under
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` autoplay starts paused, the cards fade
between slots instead of travelling, and the centre card does not grow.
::
